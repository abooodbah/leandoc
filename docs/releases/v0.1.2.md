# LeanDoc v0.1.2

LeanDoc 0.1.2 lays out pages the way Word does for papers in two columns. A
12-page IEEE-style paper written by pandoc showed as 18 pages in 0.1.1; it
now shows as 12 pages, as in Word, with each page starting close to where
Word's does.

## Release assets

| Platform | Artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11, x64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.2-windows-x64.zip** |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and compatible, x86-64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.2-linux-x86_64.deb** |
| macOS 12 or later, Intel and Apple silicon | **LeanDoc-v0.1.2-macos-universal-preview.zip** |

Every artifact has an adjacent **.sha256** sidecar. The macOS preview also has
a **.metadata.json** file that records how it was signed.

## Fixes

- **Opening long documents.** Splitting a document into pages laid out the
  whole rest of the document again at every page, in one step that froze the
  window. A 62-page supplement with 52 tables took 21 seconds to open in
  0.1.1. Each page now takes its text from a queue, so a split lays out about
  one page, and splitting runs in short slices between which the window
  responds. The supplement opens in 2.5 seconds, and no step holds the window
  for more than 0.45 seconds (the renderer's own reading of the file). Page
  breaks are the same as before this change on every document checked: 83
  documents, from the author's own papers to generated tables of 4,000 rows.
- **Resizing the window.** Fit and automatic zoom now follow a resize once it
  settles for 150 ms, instead of laying out the whole document again on every
  frame of a drag.
- **Columns.** A two-column section that runs past a page fills the left
  column and then the right, down to the bottom margin. A section that ends
  on the page has its columns balanced, and the last section of the document
  is not, as in Word. 0.1.1 balanced every section, so each page held about
  half of what Word puts on it.
- **Paragraphs split between lines.** A paragraph that reaches the bottom of
  a page continues at the top of the next one instead of moving whole. Copy
  all text still gives it as one line.
- **Footnotes.** A footnote prints at the foot of the page that refers to it,
  under a short rule. 0.1.1 put every footnote on the last page.
- **Text size and line spacing.** Headings take their own style's size, not
  the size of the linked "Heading 1 Char" style. Text in a paragraph that
  names no style takes the Normal style's size. Single line spacing is Word's
  single line rather than the bare font size, and a 9 pt abstract is spaced
  as 9 pt, not as the 10 pt of the paragraph around it.
- **Hyphenation.** Text is hyphenated only when the document turns automatic
  hyphenation on. 0.1.1 hyphenated every document.
- **Section breaks.** The empty paragraph that carries a continuous section
  break takes no space, as in Word.
- **Tables.** A table of automatic width is fitted to its content. A table's
  outer borders are drawn on its edges and inside borders between its cells,
  so a table ruled above, below, and under its header no longer shows a rule
  under every row. Cells have no top or bottom padding unless the document
  sets it. A table split across pages keeps its column widths.
- **Equations.** A subscript or superscript made of several runs, such as
  E with the subscript "sess", sets on one line instead of one letter per
  line.

## Measurements

| Document | Word | 0.1.1 | 0.1.2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IEEE-style paper written by pandoc (734 paragraphs, 9 tables, 128 equations, 13 two-column sections) | 12 pages | 18 pages | 12 pages |
| Generated two-column test document (tests/reader/make-columns-docx.mjs) | 5 pages | 10 pages | 5 pages |

On the paper, every page of 0.1.2 starts within a fifth of a page of where
Word's starts; the first six are within a tenth. In the generated document
the long two-column section has 397 lines in Word and 397 in 0.1.2.

Time to open in page view, measured in the reader's browser on the same
machine (Chromium, not the WebView2 window, which can differ):

| Document | 0.1.1 | 0.1.2 | Longest step in 0.1.2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 62-page landscape supplement, 52 tables, 1.2 MB | 21.4 s | 2.5 s | 0.45 s |
| Generated 4,000-row table | 27 s | 2.8 s | 0.9 s |

## Known gaps

- Switching between page view and reading view reads the file again, which
  takes about half a second on a 1.2 MB document, and reading view lays out
  the whole document at once (about 0.4 seconds on the same file).
- The renderer reads the file in one step that cannot be interrupted: 0.45
  seconds on a 1.2 MB document, longer on larger ones.
- Line breaks follow the browser's text layout, so a page can start a few
  lines earlier or later than in Word, and the difference can add up over a
  long document.
- Single line spacing uses the line height of Times New Roman and Arial. A
  document in Calibri is spaced about 5 percent tighter than Word.
- In a two-column section, a footnote spans the page width; Word sets it in
  the column of its reference. Footnote numbers show as list numbers.
- Find does not match a phrase that runs across a paragraph split between
  two pages.
- Documents of more than 60 pages that already carry page markers are shown
  as before.

## Installing

On Windows, run the installer from the extracted ZIP:

```powershell
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\installer\install.ps1 -OpenDefaultAppsSettings
```

Installing over 0.1.1 keeps your settings and file associations.
