# LeanDoc v0.3.0

LeanDoc 0.3.0, a free Word (.docx) viewer for Windows, Linux and macOS, lays
pages out closer to Microsoft Word. Floating tables and figures sit where
Word puts them, two-column papers keep their columns, and contents pages keep
their dot leaders. Some documents open more slowly; the numbers are below.

## Release assets

| Platform | Artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11, x64 | **LeanDoc-v0.3.0-windows-x64.zip** |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and compatible, x86-64 | **LeanDoc-v0.3.0-linux-x86_64.deb** |
| macOS 12 or later, Intel and Apple silicon | **LeanDoc-v0.3.0-macos-universal-preview.zip** |

Every artifact has an adjacent **.sha256** sidecar. The macOS preview also has
a **.metadata.json** file that records how it was signed.

## Changed

- **Floating tables, figures, and text boxes.** A box that Word floats at the
  top or foot of a page, with the text above and below it, is placed there
  across the columns. The text no longer runs over it, and the box stays on
  the page of the paragraph it belongs to.
- **Two columns.** Two-column papers, such as IEEE papers, lay out in
  columns. The references stay in two columns with a hanging indent.
- **Tab stops and leaders.** LeanDoc sizes every tab itself, with the hanging
  indent as a stop. Contents pages keep their dot leaders and right-aligned
  page numbers.
- **Numbered headings.** Headings take Word's indents and stops, and a
  centred label such as "I." is centred as in Word, with the gap after it.
- **Page numbers.** A section without a header of its own takes the previous
  one, so page numbers continue. The number takes its field code's size and
  sits at the right margin.
- **Footnote marks.** A footnote with the author's own mark shows that mark
  and no extra number.
- **Long equations.** An equation wider than its column is cut after commas
  and before operators, and wraps inside the column.
- **Hyphens at page breaks.** A page that starts inside a word ends with a
  hyphen.
- **Small capitals.** Drawn at Word's size, in the run's own font.
- **Strict OOXML.** Files saved in Word's Strict format keep their styles,
  lists, notes, and tab stops. Before, they opened without styles.

## How close to Word

Measured on 57 real-world documents, with Word 16's page counts and page
starts read through COM:

| | 0.2.0 | 0.3.0 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Same page count as Word | 30 of 57 | 34 of 57 |
| Pages that start on Word's line | 502 of 1,181 | 524 of 1,181 |

Most pages still start on a different line from Word's, because justified
line breaking drifts from Word's in long paragraphs.

## Speed and memory

Headless runs on one Windows laptop, five alternating rounds, medians:

- A 19-page two-column paper opened 6 to 11 percent slower than in 0.2.0
  across two runs (about 66 to 108 ms).
- Strict OOXML files opened about 18 percent slower and used 27 to 40 MiB
  more memory, because they now get their styles, lists, and notes.
- The 300-page and 1,000-page test reports and a document with 20 MB of
  pictures opened within 3 percent of 0.2.0, with the same memory.

## Known gaps

- Only boxes that Word wraps top and bottom are placed. Square and tight
  wrapping, and DrawingML anchors, look as they did in 0.2.0.
- A column break does not start the next column.
- Equation fonts are wider than Word's Cambria Math, so a cut equation can
  take more lines than in Word.
- A tab leader is a row of the leader character cut to the tab's width, not
  spaced exactly as Word spaces it.
- A footnote with its own mark still counts in the numbers of later notes.
- Charts, SmartArt, and other text boxes are still not displayed; the
  fidelity bar names them.

## Installing

On Windows, run the installer from the extracted ZIP:

```powershell
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\installer\install.ps1 -OpenDefaultAppsSettings
```

## Signing and privacy

The Windows programs in the GitHub ZIP are not signed.

Privacy policy: This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.
LeanDoc's own code makes no network requests and never fetches anything a
document points to. The page is drawn by the system web runtime (the Microsoft Edge WebView2
Runtime on Windows, a Microsoft component), which may contact its vendor on
its own. The full policy, including how to uninstall, is in the
[README](https://github.com/abooodbah/leandoc#privacy-policy).
