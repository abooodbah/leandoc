# LeanDoc v0.1.3

LeanDoc 0.1.3 breaks pages where Word does in documents that Word saved. A
46-page survey with tracked changes showed as 87 pages in 0.1.2. A copy of
it with every letter scrambled, which Word 365 lays out on 48 pages, now
shows as 49 instead of 87.

## Release assets

| Platform | Artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11, x64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.3-windows-x64.zip** |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and compatible, x86-64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.3-linux-x86_64.deb** |
| macOS 12 or later, Intel and Apple silicon | **LeanDoc-v0.1.3-macos-universal-preview.zip** |

Every artifact has an adjacent **.sha256** sidecar. The macOS preview also has
a **.metadata.json** file that records how it was signed.

## Fixes

- **Pages in documents Word saved.** Word saves where each page started when
  it last laid the document out, and the renderer starts a page at each of
  those places. When LeanDoc's text ran a few lines past a page, those lines
  took a page of their own, so the survey had about twice Word's pages. Text
  that runs past such a page start now continues on the page that starts
  there. Explicit page and section breaks are unchanged.
- **An empty page after a page break.** Word also saves a page start right
  after an explicit page break; LeanDoc broke at both and showed a page with
  one empty line. It now breaks once.
- **Complex-script theme fonts.** Text in the `majorBidi` or `minorBidi`
  theme font printed in the body font. It now uses the theme's complex-script
  font, or the theme's font for the document's bidi language when that is
  empty: Times New Roman for Arabic in Office's themes.
- **Line height.** A paragraph whose text and paragraph mark are smaller than
  its style was spaced at the style's size: 10 pt references in a 12 pt
  style took 18.4 px lines instead of 15.4. An empty paragraph now takes the
  size of its paragraph mark.
- **Contextual spacing.** The space between paragraphs of one style that ask
  for contextual spacing, such as a list of references, is dropped, as in
  Word.
- **Fixed tables.** A table with a fixed layout keeps its column widths.
  It was fitted to its content, which wrapped narrow columns and made rows
  taller.
- **Deleted paragraphs.** A paragraph whose text and paragraph mark were
  deleted as tracked changes showed as an empty line. It is gone, as in Word.
  The survey had 129 of them.

## Measurements

| Document | Word | 0.1.2 | 0.1.3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Survey with its letters scrambled (2,048 paragraphs, 9 tables, 4,832 tracked changes as leandoc-inspect counts them) | 48 pages | 87 pages | 49 pages |
| Generated document of 10 saved pages, each 2 lines longer than a page, with one page break (tests/reader/make-saved-pages-docx.mjs) | | 21 pages | 12 pages |

The Word figure is the owner's count in Word 365. LeanDoc's figures were
measured in the reader's browser (Chromium), not in the WebView2 window.

Page breaks are unchanged on 86 of 88 documents compared page by page with
0.1.2: the feature fixtures, 50 public test documents, and generated
documents of up to 4,000 table rows. In the other two, emptied paragraphs
with deleted marks are gone, and empty paragraphs with a 16 pt mark are
16 pt tall instead of 11 pt; their page counts are unchanged.

## Known gaps

- On the scrambled survey, 8 of Word's 45 saved page starts begin a page in
  the same paragraph in LeanDoc. Its scrambled letters are wider than the
  original text, so the text runs on past the pages Word saved for the
  original.
- An empty paragraph is as tall as its font size, not a full line as in
  Word.
- A paragraph whose mark is deleted but which keeps text is not joined to
  the paragraph after it.
- Page starts that Word saved inside a table do not start a page.
- Documents of more than 60 pages that already carry page markers are shown
  as before.

## Installing

On Windows, run the installer from the extracted ZIP:

```powershell
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\installer\install.ps1 -OpenDefaultAppsSettings
```

Installing over 0.1.2 keeps your settings and file associations.
