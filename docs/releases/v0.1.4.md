# LeanDoc v0.1.4

LeanDoc 0.1.4 breaks pages where Word does in many more documents. On 35
documents measured against Word 365 on the same machine, 22 now have Word's
page count (10 in 0.1.3) and 30 are within one page. The pages off, summed
over all 35, fell from 293 to 19. A 45-page survey that showed as 47 pages
now shows as 45.

## Release assets

| Platform | Artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11, x64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.4-windows-x64.zip** |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and compatible, x86-64 | **LeanDoc-v0.1.4-linux-x86_64.deb** |
| macOS 12 or later, Intel and Apple silicon | **LeanDoc-v0.1.4-macos-universal-preview.zip** |

Every artifact has an adjacent **.sha256** sidecar. The macOS preview also has
a **.metadata.json** file that records how it was signed.

## Fixes

- **Long tables.** Rows break across pages between lines, header rows repeat
  on each page, and rows the document keeps whole move whole. A row taller
  than a page used to hold every row after it on one page: an 11-column
  table that Word lays out on 17 pages took about 11.
- **Title pages.** In a section with its own first-page header and footer,
  the first page's footer was copied onto every page. An ACM template's
  first-page footer of 17 empty lines took about 3 lines from every page.
- **Missing fonts.** A font that is not installed is drawn in the stand-in
  the document's font table names, as Word does. Aptos, Office's default
  font, is drawn in Calibri spaced to Aptos's width; Arial, the old stand-in,
  ran 5.6% wide.
- **Line heights.** Superscripts and subscripts no longer make their lines
  taller, and an empty paragraph is one line of its mark, as in Word.
- **Numbered paragraphs** break across pages like any other, without a second
  number.
- **Saved page starts.** A page start Word saved right after a continuous
  section break is honored; it had made a blank page before a figure.

## Speed and memory

Opening the 1,000-page test report takes the same time and memory as 0.1.3:
a long document's pages get Word's line heights as they come near the
window, and the values come from shared style rules, not one inline style per
paragraph.

## Known gaps

- Word's justification fits words onto narrow justified lines slightly
  differently; a two-column test still differs on its last page.
- About a third of the measured documents are still a page or more off.
- SmartArt is not shown, and floating shapes take no space.
